Kheirie has held three data science roles. At QuantCube Technology in Paris, France, Kheirie was a Junior Data Scientist (work-study) from October 2023 to October 2025. ## NLP projects
Kheirie's documented NLP work spans three roles. At QuantCube Technology, within the NLP team, Kheirie built PMI nowcasting indicators using BERT-based sentiment analysis of Arabic and English text, with 80% correlation with the official Egyptian PMI and 88% correlation with the official Saudi Arabian PMI. At QuantCube Technology, within the NLP team, Kheirie also explored and designed agentic RAG approaches to explain variations in economic indicators. At Sanofi, in the research and development (R&D) NLP team, Kheirie was part of a team that fine-tuned BERT-based models for extractive summarization, reaching a ROUGE-1 score of 47% and a ROUGE-2 score of 25%. At Braincities Lab, Kheirie fine-tuned the Sentence-BERT model for the NLP-based ENEL job-matching project, which achieved 90% accuracy.
