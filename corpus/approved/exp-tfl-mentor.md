## Role overview
Kheirie worked as a Temporary Educational Mentor at Teach For Lebanon in Beirut, Lebanon, from October to December 2016, after two years as a Teach For Lebanon Fellow.

## Coaching and supervising teaching fellows
As a Temporary Educational Mentor at Teach For Lebanon, Kheirie supervised and coached 13 teaching fellows, supporting their lesson planning and classroom practice.

## Lesson plan review and observation reports
As a Temporary Educational Mentor at Teach For Lebanon, Kheirie reviewed the teaching fellows' weekly lesson plans and wrote monthly observation reports with development feedback.
