## Article overview
Kheirie wrote "The Foundations of Deep Learning: From Forward Propagation to Backpropagation", published on Medium in Data Science Collective on August 18, 2025. The article aims to simplify the fundamental mechanisms of neural networks and show how each component contributes to learning.

## Neural network layers and forward propagation
In the article "The Foundations of Deep Learning", Kheirie explains the input, hidden, and output layers of an artificial neural network and how data flows forward through them, with neurons applying weights, biases, and activation functions. The article uses a worked example of deciding whether to wear a coat.

## Loss functions and backpropagation
In the article "The Foundations of Deep Learning", Kheirie explains how a loss function, such as binary cross-entropy, scores the distance between a prediction and the target. It then explains backpropagation: the error moves backward through the network, the chain rule computes gradients, and an optimizer updates the weights and biases.
