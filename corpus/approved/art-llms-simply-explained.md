## Article overview
Kheirie wrote "Large Language Models Simply Explained: What They Are and How They Came to Be", published on Medium in Data Science Collective on June 25, 2025. The article's stated objective is to give a clear and broad overview of what large language models (LLMs) are and how they came to be, for a general audience.

## How large language models work
In the article "Large Language Models Simply Explained", Kheirie uses the analogy of a librarian with superpowers to explain LLMs. The article covers their three core components: a Transformer architecture, large training datasets, and training by predicting the next token. It clarifies that LLMs generate text by estimating likely next tokens rather than retrieving stored passages.

## Limitations of large language models
In the article "Large Language Models Simply Explained", Kheirie describes the limitations of LLMs: confident but incorrect output, weak precise reasoning, inherited biases, copyright concerns, and heavy computing and energy demands.

## Historical milestones of large language models
In the article "Large Language Models Simply Explained", Kheirie traces the milestones from ELIZA through RNNs, LSTMs, and GRUs to the 2017 Transformer, and then to GPT, BERT, and later models.
