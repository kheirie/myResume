## Role overview
Kheirie worked as an NLP Data Scientist at Sanofi in Chilly-Mazarin, France, from November 2021 to March 2022, on an interim (temporary) placement. At Sanofi, Kheirie was part of the research and development (R&D) NLP team. Kheirie was part of a team working on extractive summarization of detailed description documents about medicines and drugs, using BERT-based models.

## About Sanofi
Sanofi is a biopharma company that discovers, develops, and delivers medicines and vaccines.

## Extractive summarization of medicine and drug documents
At Sanofi, Kheirie was part of a team that fine-tuned and evaluated BERT-based models for extractive summarization of scientific documents: detailed description documents about medicines and drugs, in Word and PDF formats. Within the team, Kheirie worked on the Word documents. The models achieved a ROUGE-1 score of 47% and a ROUGE-2 score of 25%.

## Text preprocessing and report generation pipelines
At Sanofi, Kheirie's team built preprocessing and post-processing pipelines using SpaCy and regular expressions (Regex), and Kheirie worked on these pipelines as part of the team. The pipelines produced summaries in JSON format and as Word reports.

## Technologies used at Sanofi
BERT, SpaCy, Regex.
