## Article overview
Kheirie wrote "The Ideas That Shaped Modern Language Models", published on Medium in Data Science Collective on October 12, 2026. The article explores the experiments and engineering choices that made large language models more capable and efficient, using a framing Kheirie calls "AI's Wonderland".

## BERT and GPT as two ways to use a Transformer
In the article "The Ideas That Shaped Modern Language Models", Kheirie explains BERT and GPT as two Transformer pretraining approaches. BERT learns bidirectional context through masked language modeling and next sentence prediction and is fine-tuned for understanding tasks. GPT uses a decoder-only design with causal attention to predict the next token, which directly supports text generation.

## Scaling laws and alignment of language models
In the article "The Ideas That Shaped Modern Language Models", Kheirie covers GPT-2, GPT-3, and scaling laws relating model size, training data, and compute to loss, including Chinchilla's finding that model size and training tokens should be balanced. The article also outlines alignment through InstructGPT's three training stages (supervised fine-tuning, reward modeling, and reinforcement learning) and links these methods to ChatGPT.

## Efficiency, multimodal models, and inference-time reasoning
In the article "The Ideas That Shaped Modern Language Models", Kheirie describes efficiency techniques such as FlashAttention and sparse Mixture-of-Experts. The article also discusses multimodal models (GPT-4, GPT-4o) and inference-time scaling (o1), noting that neither guarantees correct answers, and closes with questions to ask about new model announcements.
