## Role overview
Kheirie worked as a Data Scientist at Braincities Lab in Paris, France, from August 2020 to August 2021. The work involved HR proof-of-concept solutions, including a Workforce Mapping Solution for ENEL, in which Kheirie was responsible for the job-matching component.

## About Braincities Lab
Braincities Lab is a Paris-based startup that uses artificial intelligence and data science to help organizations build teams and to help cities understand the strengths of their populations. Its HR-related features include career path prediction, team building, people-team matching, people-job offer matching, and mining text for organization culture, personality, and expertise level.

## HR proof-of-concept solutions
At Braincities Lab, Kheirie contributed to HR proof-of-concept solutions, including a Workforce Mapping Solution for ENEL Company.

## Job-matching project for ENEL
At Braincities Lab, Kheirie was responsible for the job-matching component of the Workforce Mapping Solution for ENEL. The goal was to match jobs from ENEL's database, held in unorganized Excel sheets, to standardized jobs in the O*NET (Occupational Information Network) and ESCO (European Skills, Competences, Qualifications and Occupations) classifications. A software engineer on the team was responsible for extracting O*NET's and ESCO's data. Kheirie did not do the data engineering and did not extract ENEL's data; Kheirie's data work on the project was web-scraping job pages with BeautifulSoup to collect job descriptions and examples. Kheirie also fine-tuned the Sentence-BERT model used for semantic matching. The job matching was an NLP-based project. The system achieved 90% accuracy, measuring how accurately it matched ENEL's jobs to the relevant O*NET and ESCO jobs.

## Technologies used at Braincities Lab
Sentence-BERT, BeautifulSoup.
