## Role overview
Kheirie worked as a Data Scientist at Braincities Lab in Paris, France, from August 2020 to August 2021. The work involved HR proof-of-concept solutions and a job-matching project for ENEL that Kheirie was responsible for.

## About Braincities Lab
Braincities Lab is a Paris-based startup that uses artificial intelligence and data science to help organizations build teams and to help cities understand the strengths of their populations. Its HR-related features include career path prediction, team building, people-team matching, people-job offer matching, and mining text for organization culture, personality, and expertise level.

## HR proof-of-concept solutions
At Braincities Lab, Kheirie contributed to HR proof-of-concept solutions, including a Workforce Mapping Solution for ENEL Company.

## Job-matching project for ENEL
At Braincities Lab, Kheirie was responsible for a job-matching project for ENEL. Kheirie collected and cleaned data using BeautifulSoup, and applied semantic search with Sentence-BERT to match employee profiles to roles. The system achieved 90% accuracy, measuring how accurately it matched employee profiles to relevant jobs.

## Technologies used at Braincities Lab
Sentence-BERT, BeautifulSoup.
